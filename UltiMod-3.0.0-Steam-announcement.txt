[h1]WARNO UltiMod 3.0.0 is out![/h1]

[b]Mod for your country![/b] 🫡

Want UltiAI and your favorite overhaul in the same skirmish? UltiMod 3.0.0 brings MODerator into the NDF editor. Open [b]Combine[/b], choose your mods, set their priority, and preview what happens when two mods change the same value. Once the preview looks right, build your combined mod with WARNO’s SDK.

Already made a combination in MODerator? UltiMod recognizes its build record, so you can check for updates or rebuild it. The editor also has a refreshed dark theme, and middle-click now closes one tab at a time.
